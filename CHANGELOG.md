# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-04)

First version.

- A vertical, full-screen feed of public TikTok videos from the creators you follow
- Our own local "For You" ranking: creators whose videos you finish or save rise, ones you skip fall
- Save videos for yourself, read comments, open a single link
- Video streams straight from TikTok's CDN through the app's own TLS layer; the Pi helper only finds the videos
- Needs the `tikie` helper (yt-dlp) on your own server; set its address and key in Settings
